Measured, not claimed

The prompt was tuned on a dev set, then frozen and scored once on a separate held-out test set. Every score sits next to “always guess the most common answer”, which is the bar a classifier has to beat.

How it works

  1. Precheck (code)
    Too short, non-Latin script, or mentions legal / refund / chargeback?
  2. Classify (LLM)
    Team, priority, sentiment, confidence and a two-line summary.
  3. Triage (code)
    Confidence < 0.7 → manual review. Angry + P1 → senior agent. Legal/refund → manager first.
  4. Retrieve + draft (LLM)
    BM25 finds the 3 closest resolved tickets; the reply is grounded in them.
  5. Escalate
    A Slack brief goes to the owning team's channel and waits for an agent to confirm it.
Guardrails in code, not just in the prompt
  • Low-confidence tickets reach the agent with no AI suggestion.
  • Drafts on legal / refund tickets can only acknowledge; any mention of a refund, credit or approval is replaced.
  • Email addresses or links the model invents are flagged for the agent.
  • Nothing is sent or posted automatically.

Stack: Python standard library only, a small task orchestrator that runs independent steps in parallel, BM25 retrieval, and a free LLM (Groq).
